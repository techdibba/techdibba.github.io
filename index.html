⚠ Not a medical device

Pulse Reader estimates heart rate by detecting tiny chest-wall vibrations from each heartbeat using your phone's accelerometer (a technique called seismocardiography). It is an experimental hobby project, not a certified medical or diagnostic tool.

  • Do not use it to diagnose, monitor, or make decisions about any medical condition.
  • Readings can be thrown off by movement, breathing, talking, loose contact, or clothing between the phone and skin.
  • It has not been validated against a clinical ECG on a large scale.
  • If you have chest pain, an irregular heartbeat, or any cardiac symptoms, contact a medical professional or emergency services — do not rely on this app.

How to get a good reading

  • Sit or lie down and stay as still as possible.
  • Place the phone flat against bare skin on your chest, screen up or down, pressed gently but firmly.
  • Hold your breath for a few seconds at a time if readings look noisy.
  • Adjust the threshold and minimum delay sliders if beats are being missed or over-counted.

About this build

This is an early prototype built to test the sensing approach before it's packaged as an installable app. All data currently stays on this device only.
